# Morocco Tourism Statistics (2026)

> Real, sourced content collected via live web search on 2026-09-09.

- **14.1 million** tourist arrivals in Morocco, January–August 2026.
- **August 2026** alone: over **2 million** tourists — the first time this
  monthly threshold has been exceeded.
- **Year-over-year growth:** +4.5% versus the same eight-month period in
  2025 (~608,000 additional visitors); August was +3% versus August 2025.
- Strong performance is attributed to summer leisure travel and demand for
  coastal and cultural destinations.
- Morocco's national tourism development roadmap (2023–2026) explicitly
  targets spreading tourism's economic benefit beyond established
  destinations (i.e., beyond Marrakech/coastal hubs) to more regions.
- The source consulted did not report specific revenue figures or a
  breakdown by source country — **this is a concrete gap**: the Regional
  MENA & Morocco Agent and the RAG base should prioritize finding
  source-market breakdowns (e.g., French, UK, Spanish, German visitor
  shares), since that breakdown is exactly what determines exposure to a
  crisis in any one source region.

## Why this matters for the Judge / RAG retrieval

This is the baseline against which any monitoring-agent claim about a
tourism slowdown or surge should be checked — e.g., a claim that "Region A
instability is hurting Moroccan tourism" is checked against a market that
was, as of the most recent data, still setting arrival records overall,
which does not rule out the claim but does mean it should be treated as a
segment-specific or forward-looking risk rather than an already-visible
drop in the aggregate numbers.
